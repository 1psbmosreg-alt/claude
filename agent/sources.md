# Источники для мониторинга

## Первичные (для проверки)
- CourtListener / RECAP — https://www.courtlistener.com
- Федеральные суды, правила и комитеты — https://www.uscourts.gov/rules-policies
- Govinfo (опубликованные решения федеральных судов) — https://www.govinfo.gov/app/collection/uscourts
- ABA Formal Ethics Opinions — https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/ethics_opinions/
- ABA Task Force on Law and AI — https://www.americanbar.org/groups/centers_commissions/center-for-innovation/artificial-intelligence/
- State Bar of California — https://www.calbar.ca.gov
- New York State Bar Association — https://nysba.org
- The Florida Bar — https://www.floridabar.org
- Congress.gov (законопроекты) — https://www.congress.gov
- NCSL: AI legislation tracker по штатам — https://www.ncsl.org

## Трекеры и аналитика
- AI Hallucination Cases Database (Damien Charlotin) — https://www.damiencharlotin.com/hallucinations/
- Ropes & Gray / Bloomberg Law и др. трекеры standing orders об ИИ
- RAILS (Responsible AI in Legal Services, Duke Law) — https://rails.legal
- Stanford RegLab / HAI — исследования точности legal-AI
